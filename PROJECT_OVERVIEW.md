# DeskTriage-Intent — Support Ticket Intent Classifier

Classifies synthetic support messages into billing, account access, refunds, order status, technical issues, shipping, and cancellation intents.

Synthetic text data only. The templates contain a small hand-written vocabulary and do not represent real customer language, languages, accessibility needs, or evolving product terminology. Treat the output as triage support only; a person should review routing and edge cases.
